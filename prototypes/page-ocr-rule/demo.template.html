When should a Page go to OCR?

acceleread reads each PDF Page's embedded text first and only runs OCR (Tesseract in the fast Extraction Profile, and as the gate on Docling's OCR in quality) when that text can't be trusted. This page lets you tune that one shared rule against real Pages: three born-digital filings, two synthetic scans, and 14 hand-made awkward Pages. Every Page has an expected outcome; the score shows what the rule gets wrong.

All signals were measured once by extract_signals.py (pypdfium2, no rendering), plus one cheap Jev question per Page: "Are most of the words in this text correctly spelled real words in some human language?" (total cost for every Page: ).

    What the measurements say

    • Character count alone can't be the rule. 30 real born-digital Pages hold only a page number (1–2 characters) or a running header (~25); OCR'ing them is wasted. A scan with a typed stamp has 51 characters. The threshold has to sit in that gap and be paired with "is the Page mostly image?".
    • Dictionary-word ratio catches junk cheaply. Born-digital English never fell below 0.78; garbled encoding scored 0.04 and a bad earlier OCR layer 0.44. But a perfect French Page scored 0.58. Below the cutoff, an English-only check would replace good French text with English-model OCR.
    • Jev rescues other languages but needs a floor. Real words → 0.78–0.97, French 0.95, junk 0.22–0.35. On 1–2 character Pages it says ~0.3, so it only runs once a Page has enough text. Phrasing mattered: of three tried, "real, correctly spelled words" separated best. Scores moved ±0.1 between runs.
    • No cheap signal separates "chart beside text" from "scanned exhibit beside text". Both have good text and ~40% image coverage. Telling them apart needs OCR on the image region (what Docling does in quality), or acceptance that fast mode misses it.
    • Image coverage must be the union of all images. One scan stored as 10 strips has no single big image.
    • Text drawn as vector shapes has no characters and no images, only paths. Born-digital near-empty Pages had ≤4 paths; the outlined Page had 48 (one per line).
